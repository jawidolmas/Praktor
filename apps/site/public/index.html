“It's not another coding agent. It's the layer above one — deterministic code makes every control-flow decision: spawn, verify, retry, deny. The model is only ever asked to do the coding itself.”

Built so you don't have to babysit it

FIG 0.1

Isolated by default

Every attempt gets its own git worktree and branch. Nothing the worker does ever touches your working tree directly.

FIG 0.2

Policy-enforced

A PreToolUse hook denies or asks about force-pushes, prod deploys, destructive SQL and committed secrets — enforced by the harness, not by asking nicely.

FIG 0.3

Actually verified

The supervisor runs your real acceptance command itself. Work is accepted because a check passed — never because the model said "done."

Praktor — a supervisor for Claude Code

One sentence in. A reviewed branch out.

Tell Praktor what you want in one sentence, then go chill. It keeps working whether your laptop is open or closed, parks itself the moment a rate limit hits and picks back up the instant it clears, and doesn't stop until there's a reviewed branch waiting for you.

Below is the whole loop, grown out from the daemon at its centre — six phases, sixty-six leaves, and the three feedback paths that make a second attempt smarter than the first.

The supervisor Praktor A persistent daemon. Closing the terminal — or the laptop — does not stop the work.
